# Comprehensive Execution & Funding Master Plan: Inner Compass

## Part 1: Product Execution Plan (Web-First to Mobile Scale)

### Phase 1: Web MVP & Philosophy Core (Months 1–3)

* **Goal:** Launch a responsive web application focusing exclusively on the cognitive engine, philosophy curricula, and intent-setting flows.
* **Core Deliverables:**
* User authentication and preference setup.
* CMS-backed philosophy audio/video modules (the "Why" framework).
* Manual check-in logs (subjective state sliders for calm, clarity, and contentment).
* Basic practice timer with ambient audio backing.



### Phase 2: Community & Manual Biometric Dashboards (Months 4–6)

* **Goal:** Introduce social accountability and progress tracking without requiring native mobile SDKs on day one.
* **Core Deliverables:**
* Intentional community circles and moderated activity feeds (sharing practice duration, intent, and streaks instead of selfies).
* Manual metric input or basic third-party webhooks (e.g., pulling sleep or recovery stats from wearable cloud APIs if available).
* Content moderation tools and automated safety filters.



### Phase 3: Mobile Native Migration & Advanced Integration (Months 7–12)

* **Goal:** Transition the platform into native mobile apps (iOS & Android) to capture passive health data.
* **Core Deliverables:**
* React Native or Flutter cross-platform wrappers incorporating native Apple HealthKit and Android Health Connect SDKs.
* Background audio session locks and strict discipline enforcers.
* Advanced analytics correlating practice consistency with physiological changes over 90 days.

## Part 2: Funding & Capital-Raising Plan

### Step 1: Pre-Seed Bootstrap & Validation (Months 1–2)

* **Target Capital:** Self-funded ($5,000 – $10,000 for hosting, domain, and initial design assets).
* **Action Items:**
* Build a high-converting waitlist landing page highlighting the rational, philosophy-first approach to yoga.
* Run small digital ad experiments on targeted channels to test user acquisition cost and capture initial email signups.
* Finalize a Figma interactive prototype showcasing the core web experience.



### Step 2: Friends & Family SAFE Round (Months 3–4)

* **Target Capital:** $25,000 – $75,000.
* **Action Items:**
* Issue standard Y Combinator Post-Money SAFEs (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) to close early contributions from personal networks and close peers.
* Use funds strictly to cover foundational cloud infrastructure (PostgreSQL, Redis, S3 CDN) and baseline web development.



### Step 3: Accelerator Applications & Angel Outreach (Months 5–6)

* **Target Capital:** $125,000 – $500,000 (via accelerator standard terms or angel syndicates).
* **Action Items:**
* Apply to top-tier programs (e.g., Y Combinator, Techstars) and digital health accelerators (e.g., Rock Health) armed with early web MVP retention metrics and waitlist conversion numbers.
* Pitch angel investors who are former operators from wellness, fitness-tech, or quantified-self companies (e.g., Whoop, Oura, Headspace).
* Position Inner Compass as a **quantified cognitive-transformation platform** rather than just a fitness app, highlighting its unique hook: logic-first conditioning paired with biometric tracking.
